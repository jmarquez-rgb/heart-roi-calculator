Section 05 · Considerations

What this calculator doesn't capture.

Honest caveats for the technical buyer. The math above is defensible at the Expected case and FMOL volume; these are the variables where the picture can shift.

01

Linearity at scale

The model assumes per-call token cost stays flat as volume grows. At enterprise scale, committed-use discounts and Vertex AI batch processing can reduce per-call cost by 30–50%. List pricing is the worst case, not the only case.

02

Token consumption variance

Real per-call cost varies 7x between a 30-second hangup and a 12-minute interpreter call. The Low/Expected/High columns bracket that variance, but distribution matters more than average for budgeting.

03

Format choice carries a token tax

HTML output for in-NCC rendering uses 3–5x more tokens than JSON. Choose the Markdown or JSON option in the format dropdown to see the savings — typically 65–75% output token reduction at the cost of requiring client-side rendering.

04

Operational overhead

Retry rates, audit logging, transcription costs, and observability infrastructure add real cost the token math doesn't capture. The 5% retry default is conservative; real production should track p95 actuals.

05

When manual still wins

Low volume + high complexity + regulated sign-off requirements can shift the math. Below roughly 500 calls/month with deeply subjective evaluation needs, a small human team may provide better judgment per call.

06

What we recommend instead

HEART AI handles 100% screening for compliance, accuracy, and behavioral patterns. Your QA team redirects from listening-and-scoring to coaching agents on what the AI surfaces. Your team gets smarter, not smaller.
